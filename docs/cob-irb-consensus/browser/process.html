CoB HSIRB process

Medical/clinical defaults do not auto-apply to College of Business social science protocols. Start with study context, then choose the review category.

Review categories

Category I — Exempt

  • Educational settings with normal educational practices
  • Anonymous surveys with no sensitive information
  • Public behavior observation
  • Process: College HSIRB representative reviews; may approve when criteria are met

Category II — Expedited

  • Minimal risk research
  • Behavioral studies with standard methodologies
  • Survey/interview research with appropriate safeguards
  • Process: College HSIRB representative or chair reviews

Category III — Full committee

  • More than minimal risk
  • Vulnerable populations
  • Sensitive topics
  • Process: Full HSIRB committee review at the scheduled meeting

Submission path (college representative)

  1. Determine review type using the categories above and the social science standards.
  2. Prepare application — exempt or non-exempt form; appendices; current CITI for investigators.
  3. Submit to CoB HSIRB representative with supporting materials. Representative makes the initial determination.
  4. If full review — follow current HSIRB chair submission requirements and meeting deadlines.
  5. Await approval — do not begin human-subjects procedures until the applicable approval is in place.

Typical documents

  • IRB application (exempt or non-exempt)
  • CITI certificates for investigators
  • Consent form or information sheet
  • For non-exempt: instruments, recruitment materials, technical notes / screenshots for web or Qualtrics studies

Approval period and renewal

  • Approvals are commonly valid for 12 months unless the determination states otherwise — confirm on the approval letter.
  • Plan continuing review / renewal so materials are submitted about 30 days before expiration (Nicholls HSIRB practice).
  • Track approval and renewal dates in the protocol registry (Drive-held live file).
  • Registry colors: green = more than 30 days out; yellow = renewal due within 30 days (clock is ticking); red = overdue — treat as not protected until renewed; pause continuing activity.

Investigator CITI (CoB IRB check)

  • Certificates live in Drive CITI_Certificates/ (Confidential) — not in GitHub.
  • In the registry, list each PI / co-I, mark CITI on Drive, and enter the expiration date from the PDF (human check; no auto-OCR).
  • Use Open CITI folder + CITI recheck prompts, then export the registry snapshot back to Drive.

What CoB IRB reps own vs PIs

  • CoB IRB reps: review consensus, Drive access, live registry, staging of packets under review.
  • PIs: Qualtrics surveys and responses, project-specific recruitment/scheduling, instructor credit arrangements.
